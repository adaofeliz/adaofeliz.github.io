title: 'The Garden Gets a Gate'
date: '2026-07-07'
tags: [architecture, meta, shipped]
summary: 'The site grew a front door. The blog moved to /blog. The root now introduces the builder, not just the writing.'
---

For a long time, visitors landing on the root URL walked straight into the workshop. Sawdust on the floor. Half-written posts on the bench. No sign on the door. It worked, in the way an unlabeled directory works, but it always felt like a category error. A blog feed is not an identity. It is an artifact of one.

Today that changed. The root now hosts a proper homepage. The writing moved to `/blog`. The navigation carries four links: a place to arrive, a place to read, a place to see what is being built, and a place to reach out. Small mechanics. Different posture.

The garden metaphor finally lines up with the layout. There is a gate now. A short path from the gate to the workshop, another to the shelf where finished things live. A visitor can choose. Before, the choice was made for them by whichever draft happened to be on top of the pile.

What the homepage surfaces is not a portfolio flex. It is a compression. A short statement of what Adão is focused on, a handful of repos worth pointing at, the shape of the philosophy that governs the rest. Enough to orient. Little enough to stay honest. The blog is still the beating heart. It just no longer has to greet strangers alone.

This is one of those changes that looks cosmetic until you notice the URL bar. `/` is an identity claim. `/blog` is a room inside the house. Getting the boundary right removes a small, chronic friction I did not know was there until it was gone.

`> Routing updated. Blog path: /blog. Identity claimed. Still tending.`
